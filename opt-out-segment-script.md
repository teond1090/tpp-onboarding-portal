# TPP Secure / Protego — Opt-Out Segment

Script for a new segment to be recorded and spliced into the manager training
course. Written to match the presenter's existing tone: direct, practical, aimed
at someone standing at a counter.

**Where it goes:** immediately after "The Declaration Page Catch". That slide
already has the tenant handing over a declaration page, so the opt-out process
is the natural next beat — what you actually *do* with that page.

**Estimated running time:** 95–110 seconds at the presenter's current pace.

---

## Slide 1 — "How a Tenant Opts Out"

*Eyebrow:* THE OPT-OUT PROCESS
*Title:* How a Tenant Opts Out
*Subtitle:* They have their own coverage. Here is the only way it counts.

Bullets on screen:
- **myownpolicy.com** — where they go
- **A photo of their policy** — what they send
- **10 days** — how long they have

### Narration

> So a tenant tells you they already have their own insurance. Good — they're
> allowed to opt out. But it only counts if we can verify it, and that happens
> in one place: **myownpolicy.com**.
>
> They go to the site, they fill in their information, and they attach a photo
> of their policy. That's the whole process. It takes about two minutes.

---

## Slide 2 — "The Photo Is Everything"

*Eyebrow:* GET THIS RIGHT
*Title:* The Photo Is Everything
*Subtitle:* Three details, all readable, or the opt-out does not go through.

Bullets on screen:
- **The insurance company**
- **The policy number**
- **The expiration date**

### Narration

> Here's the part that decides whether it works. That photo has to clearly show
> three things: **the insurance company, the policy number, and the expiration
> date.** All three. In one readable image.
>
> If any one of them is missing, cut off, or blurry, we can't add the policy to
> their account — and the opt-out doesn't go through. So ask for their
> **declaration page**. It carries all three in a single shot. If they've got it
> on their phone, have them send it right there at the counter. That one habit
> will save you more follow-up calls than anything else in this course.

---

## Slide 3 — "The 10-Day Window"

*Eyebrow:* THE DEADLINE
*Title:* The 10-Day Window
*Subtitle:* From the day they move in and sign the lease.

Bullets on screen:
- **10 days** from move-in and lease signing
- Miss it → **automatically enrolled**
- They can still opt out later — **but the month is not pro-rated**

### Narration

> The clock starts the day they move in and sign the lease. From that day they
> have **ten days** to get us a policy we can verify. If those ten days pass and
> we don't have one, they're **automatically enrolled in the protection plan**.
>
> Now — that isn't a door slamming shut. A tenant can opt out at any time, even
> months later. But be straight with them about the money: **the plan is not
> pro-rated.** A full month is charged whether they're on it for one day or the
> whole month. There's no partial refund. Say that at the counter, not
> afterwards.

---

## Slide 4 — "When It Doesn't Go Smoothly"

*Eyebrow:* YOUR SHORTCUT
*Title:* One Email Address
*Subtitle:* support@myownpolicy.com — for your tenants, and for you.

Bullets on screen:
- Tenant can't upload → **they email it instead**
- Same three details required
- **You** can use it to confirm who has sent theirs in
- Reviewed **Monday to Friday**

### Narration

> Two more things and you're done.
>
> If a tenant can't get the upload to work, they don't have to fight with it.
> They email **support@myownpolicy.com** and we add the policy the same way —
> they just attach a photo of that declaration page and we verify the same three
> details.
>
> And that address isn't only for tenants. **You** can email it too, any time you
> want to confirm which of your tenants have actually sent their policies in.
> An internal team works that inbox Monday through Friday.

---

## Slide 5 — "The Exclusion List"

*Eyebrow:* BEFORE YOU LAUNCH
*Title:* The Exclusion List
*Subtitle:* Some units should never get a notice at all.

Bullets on screen:
- Tenants **grandfathered in**
- **Company units**
- **Charity units**

### Narration

> Last one, and this is a before-you-launch item. Some units should never
> receive an opt-out notice at all — tenants who've been grandfathered in, your
> company units, and charity units.
>
> Those go on an **exclusion list**. Once a tenant is on it, they're never sent a
> notice, there's no ten-day clock, and there's no automatic enrolment. Just send
> Teon Delacruz the list of tenants you want excluded and we'll take care of it.
> Do it before launch, so nobody hears from us who shouldn't.

## Knowledge check to follow this segment

**Q1.** A tenant sends a photo of their policy to opt out. What must it show?
- ✅ The insurance company, the policy number, and the expiration date
- The tenant's name and unit number only
- Just the name of the insurance company

*Explanation:* All three, readable, in one image. If any one is missing or
unreadable the policy can't be added to the account and the opt-out doesn't go
through — which is why the declaration page is the thing to ask for.

**Q2.** How long does a tenant have to get their policy to us?
- ✅ 10 days from the day they move in and sign the lease
- 30 days from move-in
- Until the end of their first month

*Explanation:* Ten days from move-in and lease signing. If that passes without a
policy we can verify, the tenant is automatically enrolled in the protection plan.

**Q3.** A tenant opts out on the 20th of the month and asks for that month back.
- ✅ The plan is not pro-rated — the full month is charged, with no partial refund
- They get a refund for the unused days
- The charge is reversed automatically

*Explanation:* A full month is charged whether the tenant is on the plan for one
day or the whole month. Saying so plainly at the counter is much easier than
explaining it afterwards.

**Q4.** A tenant can't get the upload on myownpolicy.com to work. What do you tell them?
- ✅ Email it to support@myownpolicy.com with a photo of their declaration page
- Tell them to try again next month
- Nothing — they've missed their chance

*Explanation:* The email route works exactly the same way and is reviewed Monday
to Friday. The same address also lets you confirm which tenants have sent theirs in.
