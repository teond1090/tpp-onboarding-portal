Getting Started
Four steps, in order. Most teams finish the training in a single sitting.
1

Watch the training for your program

Head to Training Videos and open the course that matches the plan you’ve signed up for. Each one is under 20 minutes and ends with a printable certificate.

2

Have every manager and CSR complete it

Anyone who talks to tenants should watch. The videos stop at short knowledge checks, so your team finishes knowing the answers to the questions tenants actually ask.

3

Review the document examples

Look through Document Examples so you know what we’ll be sending, and when. We prepare all of it — nothing here needs filling in.

4

Approve your tenant letters

We’ll draft your tenant announcement letters and send them over for approval. Once you sign off, they go out from your own property management software.

Manager & CSR Certification

Training Videos

Three complete courses, each presented start to finish with built-in knowledge checks. Watch the one that matches your program — or all three if you offer more than one. Each ends with a printable completion certificate.

Each course opens in a new tab. You can pause, rewind and fast-forward freely — the video simply stops at each knowledge check until it’s answered. At the end, each person gets a printable completion certificate — and a one-click button to report their completion to us, so your training record builds itself.
